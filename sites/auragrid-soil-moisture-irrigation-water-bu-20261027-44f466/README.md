# AuraGrid: Soil Moisture & Irrigation Water Budget Planner

AuraGrid is a precision agricultural and gardening water budgeting utility designed for urban farmers, homesteaders, and enthusiastic gardeners. It calculates exact watering schedules, soil retention limits, and water volume requirements based on physical bed dimensions, soil types, crop categories, and climate conditions.

## Features
- **Bed & Soil Modeling**: Configure width, length, depth, soil composition (Sandy, Loam, Clay, Potting Mix), and mulch layers.
- **Crop & Climate Profiles**: Factor in crop water demand (Heavy, Moderate, Drought-tolerant, Seedlings), daily temperatures, sun exposure, and recent rainfall.
- **Real-Time Calculations**: Computes total soil volume, per-session water volume in gallons, recommended watering frequency, and weekly total water budgets.
- **Interactive 7-Day Forecast & Chart**: Visualizes soil moisture retention curves and schedules.
- **Actionable Pro-Tips**: Dynamic gardening advice tailored to your soil and crop setup.
- **Local Persistence & Backup**: Automatically saves your garden layout in localStorage with one-click JSON export and import.

## Local Run Instructions
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Adjust your garden bed parameters to instantly plan your irrigation schedule.

## Technologies
- Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.
- Local-first design with zero external network requests or dependencies.
