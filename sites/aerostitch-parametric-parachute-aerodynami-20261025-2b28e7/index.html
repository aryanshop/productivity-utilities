Assembly & Sewing Instructions

  • Seam Allowance: Add 1.5 cm (5/8") to all gore side margins for French seams or flat-felled seams.
  • Apex Reinforcement: Cut a circular reinforcement patch (diameter $2 imes$ vent hole) from 70D nylon at the apex to prevent tearing under tension.
  • Hemming: Fold bottom skirt hem by 1 cm and stitch with reinforced zigzag or double needle.
  • Shroud Lines: Use Kevlar or Spectra cord rated at least $3 imes$ calculated max dynamic load. Shroud line length should equal nominal diameter ($D_0$).
