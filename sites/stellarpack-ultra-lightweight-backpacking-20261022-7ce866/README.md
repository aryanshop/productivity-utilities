# StellarPack: Ultra-Lightweight Backpacking & Gear Rigidity Analyzer

StellarPack is a precision client-side productivity utility designed for hikers, backpackers, thru-hikers, and travelers to manage their gear inventory, evaluate base weight thresholds, analyze packing volume requirements, and simulate ergonomic center of gravity (COG) to prevent back strain.

## Key Features
- **Comprehensive Gear Inventory Management**: Add, categorize, and track items by weight and volume.
- **Dual Unit Support**: Instant toggle between Metric (grams/kg) and Imperial (ounces/lbs).
- **Base Weight & Rating Analysis**: Automatically calculates base weight and categorizes your kit into Ultralight (< 4.5kg), Lightweight (< 6.8kg), and Traditional.
- **Pack Volume & Capacity Sizing**: Computes total packed volume and recommends backpack liter capacity.
- **Ergonomic Center of Gravity (COG) Visualizer**: Interactive canvas simulation mapping gear weight distribution against spine alignment to optimize stability.
- **Presets & Export**: Quickly load Ultralight or Weekend preset inventories or export your gear list as JSON.
- **100% Static & Private**: Runs entirely in your browser with zero external dependencies, network calls, or data collection.

## Local Run Instructions
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser.
