# AuraCalc: Financial Compound Interest & FIRE Retirement Planner

AuraCalc is a precision financial independence, retire early (FIRE) and compound interest planner built for modern wealth builders. It runs entirely client-side with zero external dependencies or network requests.

## Features
- **Interactive Sliders & Inputs**: Instantly adjust current age, target retirement age, savings rate, expected annual returns, and inflation rates.
- **FIRE Target Calculation**: Automatically computes your target corpus based on annual spending and your chosen safe withdrawal rate (SWR).
- **Real vs. Nominal Growth**: Accounts for inflation to show your true purchasing power over decades.
- **Canvas Wealth Trajectory Chart**: Responsive HTML5 visual graph mapping your net worth accumulation against your FIRE threshold.
- **Year-by-Year Ledger**: Detailed breakdown table of contributions, investment gains, nominal net worth, and inflation-adjusted real net worth.
- **CSV Report Export**: Download your complete projection data instantly.

## Local Run Instructions
1. Download or clone repository files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`).
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Adjust sliders and inputs to model your personal financial independence path.
